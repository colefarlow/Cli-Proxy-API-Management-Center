# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Cole operates coding agents across multiple machines. He wants to check subscription allowance quickly and manage local and remote proxy hosts from a desktop app.

## Product Purpose

Make account names, remaining allowance, reset timing, and proxy setup health easy to understand while preserving every original management control. The app is a management client. Its host picker changes only the view; the app never owns the running proxy or changes T3 routing automatically.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clear, personal. Preserve the white canvas, mint mark, soft coral-to-mint accents, and quiet divisions from Cole's supplied reference. Prioritize the task over decorative metrics.

## Anti-references

Cole dislikes standalone website dashboards. Avoid unexplained icon-only navigation, oversized counts that dominate quota information, repeated technical status indicators, tiny low-contrast labels, and controls that imply a provider or host was switched when only the management view changed.

## Design Principles

- Put allowance and reset timing beside the account name.
- Offer Dashboard, Accounts, and Advanced as the daily navigation.
- Expose one health summary and keep the detailed checks available.
- Preserve all original controls and user-entered account names.
- Leave active proxy processes, credentials, and conversations in place.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use visible navigation labels, descriptive accessible names, keyboard-operable actions, readable supporting text, and sufficient text contrast. Respect reduced motion. Unknown readings stay unknown; elapsed reset times do not imply a confirmed quota refresh.
